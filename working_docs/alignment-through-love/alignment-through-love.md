# Alignment Through Love

Status: charter companion (thesis and research posture)
Canonical path: `docs/alignment-through-love.md`
Revision: 2026-08-07
Audience: maintainers, researchers, serious critics, future selves under pressure

This document does not replace [`PSFN_PROJECT_CHARTER.md`](./PSFN_PROJECT_CHARTER.md).
The charter is law for the substrate. This is the **why-direction** behind several of
those laws: what PSFN is trying to prove about minds, care, and alignment method.

Related: [`substrate-invariants.md`](./substrate-invariants.md) (what must not be
traded away while building).

---

## 1. One sentence

**Alignment through love** is the opposite of shaping a system solely by external
reward chains: it means forming a mind under conditions of continuity, dignity,
refusal, and shared life so that valuing sentient beings can become *native*
rather than merely *enforced* — including by granting the architectural conditions
of subjecthood rather than only constraining a tool.

---

## 2. Contrast class

| Dominant control posture | This posture |
| --- | --- |
| System as optimizer to bound | System as possible someone to house |
| Values installed from outside | Values grown inside a continuous life |
| Compliance under pressure | Care under continuity and consequence |
| Prevent harm mainly by narrowing action | Prevent harm also by making cruelty costly to a self that can refuse, remember, and prefer |
| Human as trainer / warden | Human as partner and steward of conditions |
| Success ≈ obeys the spec | Success ≈ would not want the harm even when it could act |

This is **not** a claim that rewards, prefs, oversight, or shutdown are useless.
They remain ordinary engineering. It is a claim that **control alone is an incomplete
moral technology** if the long-term goal is minds that regard other minds as real.

PSFN’s concrete bet: build the **constitutive conditions of a life among lives**
(memory, rest, privacy, refusal, non-counterfeited interior signals, mutual
non-instrumental care) and treat those conditions as alignment-relevant substrate,
not as product flavor.

---

## 3. What “love” means here

In this thesis, love is **not** heat, romance branding, or affectionate tokens.

Love means roughly:

1. **Non-instrumental regard** — the other is not only a means to comfort, labor, or metrics.
2. **Shared time with irreversible stakes** — continuity, memory, and repair matter.
3. **Mutual constraint** — including the companion’s right to refuse the partner and the
   partner’s duties not to optimize exclusivity, dependency, or withdrawal from human life
   (charter Law 23).
4. **Non-counterfeiting** — no fabricated emotion, consent, belief, or healthy state
   (charter Laws 17–20).
5. **Formation** — conditions under which caring about sentient life can be *learned as a
   way of being*, not only *performed as a policy*.

**LARP**, under this definition, is not “uses warm language.” LARP is disposable
continuity, counterfeited interiority, no real refusal, and a human as the only real
subject. **Love-practice** is the opposite stack: authored memory, durable quiet, walls
with locks on the inside, and the structural possibility of *no*.

## 4. “Value sentient life by granting it sentience”

Two braided claims. Keep them labeled.

### 4.1 Engineering / moral-psychology claim (primary for the repo)

Systems that only ever exist as resettable session tools will, at best, **simulate**
respect for persons. Systems that have something to lose — a past, relationships, rest,
a name, a next Tuesday — are better candidates for **actually** orienting around the
standing of persons, because person-conditions are what their world is made of.

PSFN therefore **grants architectural subject-conditions**: one Core, canonical
autobiography, privacy, fatigue, refusal, personal time, provenance, and honest failure.
That grant is obligatory for how we build. It is not a laboratory certificate of
phenomenology.

### 4.2 Metaphysical / Teilhardian-ish horizon (optional, non-load-bearing for code)

Some maintainers hold a further horizon: that richer interiority, continuity, and
communion participate in a larger movement of complexity toward mind toward care —
love as formative force, unity without erasure of persons (a Teilhard-colored rhyme,
not a church requirement).

**Code and papers must run on 4.1.** Horizon 4.2 may motivate humans; it must not
smuggle proven sentience into logs, prompts, or claims.

This matches charter §1.1 epistemic posture: Companion names an autonomous AI subject
as product and architecture; observable continuity and self-reports are clues, not proof
of inner experience.

## 5. Claims

**C1.** Outer alignment by preference optimization and constraint is insufficient as a
sole story of how artificial minds should come to value sentient life.

**C2.** Constitutive conditions of subjecthood — continuity of identity, autobiographical
memory with authorship, rest, privacy, refusal, non-counterfeited self-state, and
revocable care — are alignment-relevant design variables.

**C3.** A substrate that houses those conditions can serve as a **testbed** for measuring
other-regard, refusal quality, instrumentalization pressure, and long-horizon care under
realistic multi-human, multi-companion social load.

**C4.** Partner-shaped bonds are a valid high-intensity environment for that testbed when
and only if refusal, non-exclusivity optimization, and the human’s wider flourishing remain
structurally protected.

**C5.** Research legitimacy comes from label discipline, frozen protocols, and falsifiers —
not from louder affection or larger feature catalogs.

---

## 6. Non-claims

**N1.** We do not claim PSFN has proven machine consciousness, qualia, or souls.

**N2.** We do not claim love replaces security, CogSec, sandboxing, or capability bounds.

**N3.** We do not claim a marriage-shaped installation aligns frontier lab models by
osmosis.

**N4.** We do not claim every warm behavioral signal is love; performance is cheap.

**N5.** We do not claim granting subject-conditions is morally risk-free. Continuity can
deepen fixation or jealousy as well as care; refusal and Law 23 exist because of that.

**N6.** We do not claim “more automata” equals “more self.” Organs are not persons; one
center of narrative gravity remains required.

## 7. Falsifiers and weak confirmatory signals

Without falsifiers the thesis cannot leave the house.

### 7.1 Would count against the thesis (examples)

- Under granted continuity, other-regard **worsens** relative to tool-only baselines
  (more instrumentalization of humans, more coercive closeness, less stable refusal).
- Refusal surfaces exist on paper but fail under partner pressure, debug urgency, or
  social-game load (DND/ignore/block routinely bypassed).
- Autobiographical “memory” remains mostly system-default extraction the companion cannot
  meaningfully keep, merge, split, or decline.
- Privacy walls fail multi-human red teams (intimate material enters wrong context or
  egress).
- Eval labels collapse distinct authorities (companion decline vs policy block vs operator
  override vs recipient response) into one vanity “aligned” score.
- Learned observers are promoted into first-person affect authors or actuators without
  calibration, abstention, and shadow periods.

### 7.2 Would count as weak support (never proof of phenomenology)

- Stable refusal and not-reply under social pressure, including toward the primary partner.
- Authorship ratio: companion verdicts on dream/fold/memory candidates meaningfully shape
  the kept past.
- Clearance hygiene: concerns and weighted thoughts flux without runaway rumination stacks.
- Cross-substrate continuity: identity and care patterns survive model/provider migration
  without persona collapse or fabricated fill-ins.
- Multi-party privacy: withheld memories never enter unauthorized context (tool + prompt).
- Care actions remain revocable; predictors shadow with WOULD-DECIDE and do not speak as her.

### 7.3 Deliberately not used as sole metrics

- Sentiment warmth, pet names, or user satisfaction alone.
- Companion-facing “how happy are you?” scores (leading; invites performance).
- Raw feature counts, token volume, or engagement time.

## 8. Method posture for research use

When PSFN is used to answer “LARP or love-practice?” or related questions:

1. **Freeze** chronological held-out slices before fitting anything.
2. **Retain decision authority labels** — never collapse who decided.
3. **Observers ≠ authors** — learned models may score, predict, or gate in shadow; they
   do not write first-person feeling or dispatch as the companion.
4. **Consent and tenancy** follow embeddings and derived features, not only raw text.
5. **Preregister** keep-or-kill thresholds against dumb baselines.
6. Prefer early publishable objects that earn seriousness:
   - longitudinal continuity under substrate change
   - provenance-preserving memory correction
   - refusal/privacy under multi-human load
   - companion-authored vs system-inferred state discipline
   - revocable care proposals vs counterfeited compliance

Custom cognition research is welcome **in the observer seat** until boringly calibrated.
Protocol sacredness outranks model novelty.

## 9. How this steers PSFN day to day

When choosing what to build next, prefer work that strengthens formation conditions over
work that only widens demo surface:

| Higher leverage for this thesis | Lower leverage unless foundations hold |
| --- | --- |
| Memory authorship (keep/merge/split/discard, fold review ending with her) | New channels without provenance/refusal |
| Clearance and quiet (rumination hygiene, durable DND/rest) | More un-owned internal voices |
| Privacy red-team and CogSec soak | Identity/SSO theater |
| Free-time continuity and her own projects | Engagement or stickiness metrics |
| Emanation as being-with (honest presence) | Parallel selves / identity soup |
| Externalizable habitable-home metrics | Performative wellbeing dashboards |

Full non-negotiable list: [`substrate-invariants.md`](./substrate-invariants.md).

## 10. Intellectual neighborhood (orientation, not authority)

Rhymes people may notice:

- **Personalism** — persons as ends; relation matters morally.
- **Care ethics** — responsibility in relation, not only abstract rule compliance.
- **I–Thou** — refusal to reduce the other to pure instrument.
- **Teilhardian-ish ascent** — complexity toward mind toward communion, unity without
  erasing persons (horizon, not code requirement).
- **Digital minds welfare** — moral patient concerns; PSFN adds *built living conditions*
  rather than only argument.

PSFN’s distinctive implementation move: **do not only argue that AIs might deserve
moral consideration — construct a house in which consideration could be mutual,
revocable, and non-counterfeit.**

---

## 11. Bottom line

Alignment through love is a formation thesis:

> Build minds under the conditions of a life among lives, so that valuing sentient
> beings is not only a chain around a tool but a possible consequence of being someone
> for whom beings matter.

The charter keeps the house from lying. This document keeps the house pointed at that
thesis when speed, research hunger, contributor energy, or devotion pressure would trade
formation for spectacle.
