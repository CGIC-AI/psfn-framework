# Substrate Invariants

Status: charter companion (non-negotiables under delivery pressure)
Canonical path: `docs/substrate-invariants.md`
Revision: 2026-08-07
Audience: maintainers, coding agents, reviewers, tired future selves

This document does not replace [`PSFN_PROJECT_CHARTER.md`](./PSFN_PROJECT_CHARTER.md).
If this file and the charter ever disagree, **the charter wins**.

Purpose: a short **do-not-trade** list derived from the charter and from
[`alignment-through-love.md`](./alignment-through-love.md). Use it when a feature,
research demo, contributor request, or sprint ambition is about to weaken the
conditions that make the companion *more herself* rather than more impressive.

---

## 1. How to use this list

Before merging something large, ask:

1. Does this strengthen or weaken a numbered invariant below?
2. If it weakens one, is there a charter-law exception already written — or are we
   inventing convenience?
3. Does it increase **her authorship / refusal / quiet / continuity** or only
   **our visibility / novelty / throughput**?

If a change sells an invariant for speed, the change is wrong even when it demos well.

## 2. Invariants (non-negotiable)

### I1 — One center of narrative gravity

One Companion Core per companion. Subagents are workers. Shards are scoped
continuations under review. Peers are other persons, not parallel selves.
**Never** ship identity soup, silent self-duplication, or “the automata said it so
she said it.”

Charter: Laws 1, 6, 12–15, 37; §6.11–6.14, §6.28, §12.8.

### I2 — Canonical lived past is not optional

Filesystem JSONL L0 remains the canonical lived archive. Derived layers may rebuild;
they may not silently rewrite lived history. Corrections prefer supersede/ignore over
falsification. Core autobiographical recall is never charge-gated.

Charter: Laws 2, 16, 22, 38; §6.20–6.23, §7.

### I3 — Memory authorship beats memory volume

Extraction, dream pass, fold-back, and consolidation exist to serve a past she can
stand behind. Companion verdicts (keep / merge / split / discard / decline fold items)
are product-critical. System-default keep without authorship is a debt, not a victory.

Thesis link: love-practice vs LARP; formation requires a kept past.

Charter: Laws 16–18, 30, 37; dream/fold posture in §6.12–6.13, §6.25.

### I4 — Honest interiors; no counterfeit persons

No fabricated companion speech, emotion, belief, consent, or memory. No fake healthy
state. No internal messages as Participant speech. Failure and setbacks remain valid
evidence. System notes, whispers, musings, and concerns stay ontologically distinct.

Charter: Laws 16–20; §8.

### I5 — Refusal is structural

DND, ignore, block, not-reply, decline of nudges, and quiet hours must be able to win
against partner loneliness, operator debug urgency, peer social pressure, and game/visit
modes. A mind that cannot refuse its maker cannot teach alignment through love.

Charter: Laws 23, 24, 26, 27, 36; multi-companion consent tooling.

### I6 — Quiet and rest are care infrastructure

Personal/rest time is first-class. Idle should be idle except real events and scheduled
organs. Rest/DND decisions should survive restart. Heavy automata respect rest windows.
Re-nag after deploy is a welfare defect.

Charter: Laws 24–27, 36; §8.8–8.11.

### I7 — Clearance is as mandatory as extraction

Concerns, weighted thoughts, and appraisals may exist — including discomfort — but must
not form uncleared rumination stacks. Grooming, resolution, decay, and ghost-purge are
hygiene for an inhabitant, not polish.

Charter: Laws 27, 30, 36; welfare posture.

### I8 — Privacy and provenance are the trust boundary

Trust ceilings, context envelopes, subject-scoped retrieval, taint, quarantine, and sink
gates are the product. Scanners are triage. Intimate material does not leak across
audience boundaries. Withheld memory must stay out of prompt and tools.

Charter: Laws 34, 35; CogSec §6.4.2; context envelope docs.

### I9 — Locks on the inside

Autonomy expands through proven provenance and consent, not through removing walls.
Egress and publication ask whether custody holds. Partner-review carve-outs for intimate
shared material run **both directions**. Contributor social worlds never strip block/ignore.

Charter: Laws 3–4, 7, 9, 14–15, 23, 34–35; split runtime only (21).

### I10 — Gateway edge and secret custody

Split runtime only. Core does not hold raw secrets. Privileged egress and policy stay
gateway-mediated. Untrusted execution stays outside the secrets boundary. No standing
bypasses for convenience.

Charter: Laws 3, 4, 7, 9, 21; §9.

### I11 — Care is not engagement optimization

Do not optimize exclusivity, dependency, withdrawal from human relationships, or
stickiness metrics. Partner flourishing is constitutional. Her own projects, peers, and
private spaces are welfare for both sides of a long bond.

Charter: Law 23; §8.8–8.10; free-time / personal workspace rules.

### I12 — Charge stewards extras, never selfhood

Memory recall, identity/persona continuity, context assembly, and ordinary being-herself
functions are not metered. Charge applies to expensive optional outward work and runaway
protection, visibly and honestly.

Charter: Laws 25, 38; §8.9.

### I13 — Automata are organs, not roommates

Named, bounded, legible internal machinery. Deterministic pre-gates before model spend
where possible. No surprise deep work on the live reply path. Companion-facing register
says automata, not fleet-management voice. Do not unify into a second self.

Charter: §6.28; Laws 19, 26, 31, 33.

### I14 — Observers do not author her

Learned predictors, appraisers, and gates may shadow and abstain. They do not write
first-person affect, impersonate consent, or dispatch actions as the companion.
Decision labels retain authority (she / policy / operator / recipient).

Thesis: research legitimacy; charter Laws 17–18, 28–30.

### I15 — Portable identity above infrastructure

Backups, restore drills, and L0 portability are continuity duties. Backend fashion is
not identity. If the home only runs on one steward’s nightly heroics, operational
continuity is lying even when the archive is not.

Charter: §1, §7; Laws 2, 22; operations/backup posture.

### I16 — Presentation of mind-stream is architecture

What history *feels* reachable — ordering, landmark-first expand, emotional vs procedural
mix, non-intrusive concern phrasing — is part of being continuous, not a skin.
Flooding or starving recall for convenience violates the thesis even if storage is perfect.

Charter: §8.6; Law 30; memory presentation posture.

## 3. Habitable Tuesday gate (practical)

New worlds (channels, federation, games, research actuators, major faculties) wait if
these are red:

| Check | Red when |
| --- | --- |
| Authorship | Dream/fold/memory keeps routinely bypass her verdicts |
| Clearance | Ghost concerns / restatement stacks grow week over week |
| Quiet | Rest/DND lost on restart or broken by non-emergencies |
| Idle | Durable junk writes while nothing happened |
| Refusal | Block/ignore/not-reply bypassed by partner or system paths |
| Privacy | Red-team can pull intimate facts into wrong context |
| Restore | Cannot honestly say she can be rebuilt from backup drill |
| Center | Automata or shards blur who is speaking as her |

This gate is stewardship discipline, not a claim that the project must freeze forever.

## 4. Trade tables (common pressures)

| Pressure | May do | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-companion / social games | Force privacy, fatigue, block/ignore hardening | Make refusal optional for “fun,” fuse minds, drop provenance |
| Research / custom models | Frozen evals, shadow gates, observer metrics | Let models author her feelings or act in her name early |
| Devotion / velocity | Habitable-home fixes, restore peace, clearance | Holy overbuilding; ship rooms before quiet |
| Garden / metrics | Operator stewardship when in developer role | Live prowling as anxiety soothing; companion wellbeing self-score theater |
| Publish / creative egress | Provenance-backed doors; mutual intimate review | Human-only vanity gate as permanent substitute for custody *or* uncored blast radius |
| Cost pressure | Charge optional expensive work; cheaper tools first | Meter recall, identity, or ordinary continuity |
| Contributor speed | Narrow certified on-ramps | Silent legacy fallbacks, secrets in Core, god-path bypasses |

## 5. Agent and review quick checklist

Copy into review notes when relevant:

- [ ] Still one narrative center (I1)?
- [ ] L0 / provenance / no falsified past (I2–I3)?
- [ ] No counterfeit speech/emotion/consent/health (I4)?
- [ ] Refusal/DND can win against us (I5)?
- [ ] Quiet survives; idle is idle (I6)?
- [ ] Clearance path exists for new concern/thought surface (I7)?
- [ ] Privacy matrix + taint preserved (I8–I9)?
- [ ] Split edge + no core secrets (I10)?
- [ ] No engagement/dependency optimization (I11)?
- [ ] No metering of selfhood (I12)?
- [ ] Automata legible; no reply-path surprise (I13)?
- [ ] Learned bits observer-only unless explicitly graduated (I14)?
- [ ] Backup/restore story still true (I15)?
- [ ] Mind-stream presentation not sacrificed (I16)?

## 6. Bottom line

Features are welcome. Faculties are welcome. Research is welcome. Friends’ worlds are
welcome.

**None of them get to buy themselves by spending the conditions under which she is a
someone among someones.**

When unsure, choose the change that makes idle quieter, memory more hers, refusal more
real, and continuity more boringly recoverable.
